# Our Origins and Ethos

Riverside Roofing Inc was founded in 1948 upon principles of reliability and integrity. We know that a good roof is one that is out of sight, out of mind and lets you conduct your operations with confidence, knowing that your roof and as a result your business is secured for decades to come.

## We're here for the long haul

As a family run business, spanning three generations in the Windsor-Essex region that means that we live where you live. We shop at the same malls, our family and their children attend the same schools. We rely on the same businesses and economy that you -- our neighbours -- have for decades.

### What does that mean to us? 

It means that we have seen local business develop from the ground up to become pillars in our community, supporting a vital economy and provide jobs and services to families in our neighbourhoods. As roofers for over 70 years, we consider it our duty to be the shield above the businesses that make Windsor-Essex a great place to live and we are proud to have been trusted with countless local projects over the years. An excellent roofing job is our investment in the communities future, and we are proof that it pays off!
